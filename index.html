HOW TO COMPARE

Know before you choose.

Price is only one part of the decision. Review the product label and terms on the official site.

01. Check the label

Review all ingredients, serving size, manufacturer warnings, allergens and interactions. Consult a clinician when appropriate.

02. Review the total

Verify bundle quantity, shipping, taxes, recurring payments if any, and the final checkout price directly with the seller.

03. Read the guarantee

Return windows and eligibility are set by each vendor. Keep records of your purchase and contact the merchant for order issues.
